01

The quiet room

HÚE Book & Cafe

A quiet corner for books and coffee.

On Lương Thế Vinh the walls are ochre and the banquettes are green. Shelves hold the afternoon. Coffee arrives with lotus seeds, and a book is already open on someone’s lap.

Pastry sits on blue-and-white porcelain. The street stays at a polite distance. It is the room for a slow morning, a chapter, or nothing in particular.

02

The alley

HÚE by Xéooo

The taste of Huế, down the alley.

Down hẻm 10 Bến Nghé, the windows are wood and the walls are stone. Pizza comes off the board. Tables fill with flowers, cake, and talk that runs past the dinner hour.

Coffee at a long table. Outside, the alley has not gone quiet.

03

The sky

HÚE Imperial
Sky Lounge & Cafe

The Perfume River, from above.

On the 16th floor of TTC Imperial Hotel, Huế arranges itself: the bridges, Cồn Hến, and the Perfume River turning gold. Salt coffee before dark. Cocktails once the city lights come on.

Koi lanterns hang over the bar. The terrace is for dresses, dates, and the long look down the old capital.
